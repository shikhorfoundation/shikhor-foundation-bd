WHO WE ARE

We believe in the power of people helping people.

Shikhor Foundation is a community-driven humanitarian initiative built on a simple belief: meaningful change begins when we care for one another. Like the roots of a tree, compassion, unity, and responsibility help communities stand strong.

Humanity

Every person deserves dignity, care, and support.

Togetherness

Collective effort builds sustainable impact.

Transparency

Trust grows through honesty and accountability.

Tagline

"Together for a Better Tomorrow."

We work tirelessly to respond to real needs with empathy, responsibility, and total transparency, ensuring every helping hand reaches the right place.

OUR DIRECTION

Mission & Vision

Our Vision

A compassionate and inclusive society where every individual has the opportunity to live with dignity, hope, and a secure future.

Our Mission

To mobilize people, resources, and ideas for practical community support, impactful educational initiatives, and meaningful social change.

OUR INITIATIVES

Turning compassion into action.

Humanitarian Assistance

Supporting people and families experiencing hardship through thoughtful winter clothing distribution, relief packages, and emergency support.

Educational Support

Empowering rural and disadvantaged students by providing essential educational materials, books, and learning guidance for a brighter future.

Community Support & Awareness

Bringing people together to identify community needs, foster cooperation, and respond with care, responsibility, and unity.
